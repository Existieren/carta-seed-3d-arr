What this means if you're writing seed checks

  • Don't anchor AI Infra valuations to ARR multiples. Comp set is bimodal: frontier labs at $0 + tooling players at $1-5M.
  • Analytics, AI Apps, Marketplace, Cybersecurity are the cleanest sectors for revenue-anchored seed comps. Trust the multiple.
  • For Biotech, Hardware, Semis, Renewables, replace the multiple with a milestone schedule. ARR is the wrong question.
